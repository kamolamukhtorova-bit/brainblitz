About BrainBlitz

BrainBlitz turns review time into a live game. A teacher hosts a quiz, students join on any device with a PIN, and the whole class races to the podium.

No accounts for students

Students only need a PIN and a nickname. Nothing to install.

Built to be accessible

Answers use shape and colour, controls work with a keyboard, and animations respect your reduced-motion setting.
